Functional Specification: Shopping Cart

Overview:
The shopping cart feature allows users to add products to their cart, modify quantities, remove items, and proceed to checkout.

Features:

1. Add to Cart
   - Users can add products to their cart from product detail pages
   - Users can add products to their cart from search results
   - Users can specify quantity when adding to cart
   - Default quantity is 1
   - Maximum quantity per product is 10

2. View Cart
   - Users can view all items in their cart
   - Cart displays product name, image, price, quantity, and subtotal
   - Cart displays total price of all items
   - Cart displays estimated shipping cost
   - Cart displays estimated tax

3. Modify Cart
   - Users can increase quantity of items
   - Users can decrease quantity of items
   - Users can remove items from cart
   - Cart updates automatically when changes are made

4. Cart Persistence
   - Cart contents are saved when user logs out
   - Cart contents are restored when user logs back in
   - Cart contents are saved for 30 days
   - Anonymous users' cart is saved using cookies

5. Checkout
   - Users can proceed to checkout from cart
   - Users must be logged in to checkout
   - Users are redirected to login page if not logged in
   - Cart is validated before checkout
   - Out of stock items are flagged before checkout
